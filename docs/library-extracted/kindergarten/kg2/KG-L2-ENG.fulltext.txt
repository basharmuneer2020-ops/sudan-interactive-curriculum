٤ 
 
 .نموذج درس نموذجي
 
 كل حصة
٠٢ 
 
 .
 دقيقة النموذج التالي لدرس «Colors: Red & Blue».
Section 1 — Opening Song (2 min)
«Hello, hello, how are you? I'm fine, I'm fine, thank you!» 
 
 
 يغني كل األطفال بحركات.
Section 2 — Flashcards (4 min)
Show red card → «Red! Red apple, red car, red flower». Show blue card → «Blue! 
Blue sky, blue water, blue bird». Repeat 3 times.
Section 3 — TPR Game (5 min)
«Touch something RED!» Children run to red objects. «Touch something BLUE!» 
They run to blue. Repeat with laughter.
Section 4 — Color Song (4 min)
«Red, red, red apple! Blue, blue, blue sky! I love red and blue, yes I do!» Children sing 
and dance.
Section 5 — Craft (4 min)
Each child colors a big circle: half red, half blue. They show it to the class saying «Red 
and Blue!»
Section 6 — Closing (1 min)
«Bye-bye song» together. Children wave and leave happily.

 نصائح تربوية
•
Use props: real objects are better than pictures.
•
Speak slowly and clearly but with natural intonation.
•
Celebrate every attempt, never correct in front of others.
•
Make a small English-only corner (stickers, books).
•
Wear a bright scarf or hat as «English teacher signal».
•
Record yourself so parents can review at home.
•
Use puppets — children talk to puppets more than teachers.
•
Have a «mystery bag» with a new item each week.
 إدارة الصف
•
Physical area dedicated to English (corner or wall).
•
Visual schedule in English.
•
Hand-signal for «listen in English now».
•
Song to mark transitions.
•
English names for children as nicknames (if they like).
